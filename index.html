Notes for production

  • Locked sentences: the offer, bag, sold-out, at-the-event and terms sentences are pasted, never reworded. They are in copy/copy.md with every subject, preheader and body.
  • Never add: what is in the bag, a price, the event's address, the words raffle, drawing, win or chance, or anything from the event page about its bar or its contest.
  • Fine print and footer: keep every line in every email. Sweed's own unsubscribe link replaces the placeholder.
  • Sold out: checked against the event page each send morning. If that list reopens, a version without the sold-out wording replaces the affected pieces.
  • Email HTML: checked in Chromium at phone width only. If Sweed does not take pasted HTML, rebuild from the graphics and the copy deck.
  • Look: espresso #1A100E, gold #ECC073, cream #F4E8D0. Cinzel (display), Montserrat (plates on the graphics), Outfit (email text): all Google Fonts.
