# Noiré × Magic Garden Botanicals · First 10 (VIP passes to the Magic Meltz launch night)

Preview: https://jc1147.github.io/noire-magic-meltz/  ·  Download everything: https://github.com/jc1147/noire-magic-meltz/archive/refs/heads/main.zip

**Hold:** final concept, built for production. Nothing is scheduled or posted until James gives the go.

- `emails/` — 3 emails. In each folder: `email.html` (its images beside it in `img/`), `phone-full.png` (full screenshot at
  phone width), `phone-screen.png` (first phone screen).
- `images/` — every graphic at full size, one folder per email: the graphic used inside the email (1200x1280), the same
  with logo (1200x1500) and the 1920x1080 version. `images/instagram/` holds the post (1080x1350) and the story (1080x1920).
- `copy/copy.md` — paste-ready words: subjects, preheaders, bodies, the blocks every email shares, the one-line additions
  for other days' emails, the one text (on hold) and the Instagram caption.
- `plan/whats-being-added.csv` — only what this campaign adds, by day. Your regular sends are not listed.

Rules that hold everywhere:
- The offer, bag, sold-out, at-the-event and terms sentences are locked: paste them, never reword them.
- Each email graphic names a relative day (NEXT WEDNESDAY, THIS WEDNESDAY, TOMORROW · 10 AM): use each one only on its send day.
  The Instagram art names no relative day.
- Keep every fine-print and footer line. Sweed's own unsubscribe link replaces the placeholder.
- "Sold out" is checked against the event page each send morning. If that list reopens, a version without the sold-out
  wording replaces the affected pieces.
- The email HTML was checked in Chromium at phone width only. If Sweed does not take pasted HTML, rebuild from the graphics
  and `copy/copy.md`.

Look: espresso #1A100E, gold #ECC073, cream #F4E8D0. Type: Cinzel (display), Montserrat (plates on the graphics), Outfit
(email text); all three are Google Fonts. The logo file is `emails/email-1_fri-10-9/img/noire-gold-boxed.png`.
